The (3,3) game

The payoff matrix every OHM fork puts on its landing page. We put the same one — and then the sentence that goes under it.
StakeBondSell
Stake(3,3)(1,3)(−1,1)
Bond(3,1)(1,1)(−1,1)
Sell(1,−1)(1,−1)(−3,−3)
If everyone stakes, everyone wins.
If everyone leaves, whoever left first won.
Both are true at once, and that tension is the protocol — not a bug in it. Every (3,3) ever shipped had this same square in the bottom-right corner. Nobody fixed it. We just stopped hiding it.
