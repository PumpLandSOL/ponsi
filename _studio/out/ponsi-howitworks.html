How it works

Three moving parts, same as every reserve protocol since 2021. Nothing here is novel — that is rather the point.
I · STAKE

Get in

Stake $PONSI, receive sPONSI. Your balance rebases up every epoch — automatic, compounding, nothing to claim. Your token count goes up. That is the whole of what it does.

→
II · BOND

Feed the treasury

Sell ETH or USDC to the protocol at a discount for vesting $PONSI. Every bond becomes protocol-owned reserve — the one part of this design that was always honest.

→
III · BACKING

The floor

The treasury backs every token. Backing is real and measurable. It is also not the price, and the gap between those two is where every (3,3) holder has ever been hurt.

The APY is an emission rate, not a return.
It pays you in more $PONSI — and more $PONSI is exactly what dilutes the price. Token count going up is not value going up. Any (3,3) that lets you believe otherwise is selling you the confusion on purpose.
